Ronda — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Operator: Timothy Goode, trading as Goodeboy

Contact: privacy@goodeboy.com

Ronda lets you keep in touch with people you have actually met while

travelling. This policy describes exactly what the app stores, where it goes

and what you can remove. It is written to match what the software does, not to

cover us for things it does not do.

The short version

  • We ask for an email address, a first name and a home country. Nothing else

is required.

  • Your location is stored and shown as a city only. Your device works out

which city you are in without sending your coordinates anywhere.

  • Nobody can find you, see you or message you unless you met them in person

and both of you scanned a code. There is no search and no friend requests.

  • One exception, and it only happens if someone you met chooses it. If

they invite you to a plan — a thing to do, at a time, at a place — the other

people in that plan can write in the shared chat, and some of them may be

people you have not met. They cannot see your profile, cannot message you

privately, and you can leave at any moment without anyone being told. The

chat always shows who invited whom, so nobody in the room is a mystery.

  • We do not sell data, we do not advertise, and there is no analytics or

tracking SDK in the app.

  • You can delete your account from inside the app, and it takes everything

with it.

What we store

To sign you in

  • Email address. Used to send a one-time sign-in code. We never send

marketing to it.

Your profile

  • First name and home country (required).
  • Optionally: a short bio (140 characters), an Instagram handle, one

link, a profile photo, and up to six photos of your trip.

Photos are stored in private buckets. They are never publicly addressable —

each one has to be signed for a specific viewer, and the server only signs it

for someone you have met.

Where you are

  • The city and country you are currently in, and when it was last updated.
  • A history of the cities you have stayed in, grouped into trips.

Your device converts its position into a city name on the device itself.

The coordinates are not transmitted to us and we never store them. We hold no

street address, no venue, no accommodation name and no live position.

When the Round map needs to place a city, the app sends the city name —

for example "Hanoi, Vietnam" — to Apple's geocoding service to get that

city's location. That is a fact about the world, not about you.

You can switch off city sharing, or hide yourself entirely, at any time in

You → Who can see you.

Plans you are part of

  • The plan: its title, time, meeting point, any note or link, and who is

going.

  • Who invited whom, which is shown to everyone in the plan.
  • The plan's chat, readable only by the people in it.

A plan does not make anyone a connection. When you leave a plan, or it is

archived, the people in it lose any visibility they had of you through it.

Who you have met

  • Connections: the person, the city you met in, and the date.
  • Private notes you write about someone. These are visible only to you —

there is no way for the other person to read them.

  • Messages and photos you exchange, readable only while you are still

connected.

To make the app work

  • Timezone and quiet hours, so a notification does not arrive at 3am.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.
  • The timestamp of your 18+ confirmation. We ask you to confirm you are

18, so we record that you confirmed and when. We do not ask for or store

your date of birth.

Safety

  • Blocks and reports, including any detail you write in a report.

Links you open

Ronda sometimes shows a link somebody has added — a booking page on a plan, a

website on a profile. If you choose to open one, we record **that you opened

it**:

  • the website's domain (example.com), and nothing else from the address
  • which screen it was on
  • the city you were in
  • the plan it belonged to, if it was on a plan
  • the time

We keep the domain only. A booking address can carry your dates, your name

and a price inside it, and none of that is stored.

Why we keep it: to find out whether this app is useful to the places

travellers actually spend money — which is how it might one day pay for

itself. Today no link earns us anything. If one ever does, it will say so

next to the link, before you tap it.

We do not record anything else about how you use the app: not which screens

you look at, not how long for, not what you tap.

What we do not collect

Precise or background location, your contacts, your photo library beyond the

images you choose, your full name, your date of birth, payment details,

advertising identifiers, or any third-party analytics or tracking software.

The one thing we record about your use of the app is described under **Links

you open** above.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Delete your account and it goes.

Deleting your account removes your profile, photos, city history, trips,

connections, messages, private notes, push tokens and the record of any links

you opened.

One thing survives: if you have been reported, the report remains so that a

pattern of behaviour cannot be erased by making a new account. Your identifier

is detached from it, so the record no longer names you.

Your choices

  • See and change everything on your profile: You → Edit profile.
  • See what others see: You → Preview.
  • Stop sharing your city, or hide entirely: You → Who can see you.
  • Turn off pings at any time.
  • Disconnect, block or report anyone from their profile.
  • Delete your account: You → Delete my account.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct,

export or restrict the processing of your data. Write to

privacy@goodeboy.com and we will respond within 30 days.

How to reach us

By email, at privacy@goodeboy.com. We answer within 30 days.

If you are not happy with how we handle a privacy matter, you can complain to

the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children

Ronda is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it, and you confirm this at

sign-up. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18 we delete it.

Where data is held

Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1). If you are outside that region your data is transferred there

to run the service.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change

takes effect.

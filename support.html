The things people ask first

How do I add someone?

In person, by scanning their code, and no other way. One of you opens Meet

and the other taps Scan to meet. There is no search, no friend requests

and no suggestions — if you did not stand in front of each other, Ronda has no

way to connect you. That is deliberate, and it is most of the point.

Someone I met isn't showing up

You will only see each other once the scan has gone through on one phone. If

the scan failed, nothing was saved on either side — try again, with the code

on screen rather than a photo of it.

What does Ronda know about where I am?

The city you are in, and nothing finer. Your phone works out the city name

itself and sends only that; your coordinates never leave the device. You can

turn city sharing off, or hide entirely, under You → Who can see you.

I'm not getting pings

A ping fires when someone you have met arrives in the city you are in, so it

needs both of you to have city sharing on and pings enabled. It is also

genuinely rare — that is what makes it worth having. Check You, and check

that notifications are allowed for Ronda in iOS Settings.

Can I use this to meet new people?

No. Ronda only ever shows you people you have already met in person. There is

no discovery, no feed and no way to browse strangers.

Reporting someone

Open their profile and choose Report. Reports are read by a person, and we

act on them within 24 hours. You can also Block, which is immediate and

silent — they are not told.

If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first. We

cannot respond faster than they can.

Deleting your account

You → Delete my account. It removes your profile, your photos, your city

history, your trips, your connections and your messages. It cannot be undone,

and we do not keep a copy.

Age

Ronda is for adults. You must be 18 or over, and you confirm this when you

sign up.

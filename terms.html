2. What Ronda is

Ronda keeps you in touch with people you have met in person while travelling.

Connections are made only by two people scanning each other's code face to

face. There is no search, no friend requests and no way to add someone by

username or link.

Plans are the one place you may share a space with someone you have not

met. Anyone in a plan can invite people they have connected with, so a group

forms the way it does in a hostel. A person in a plan with you is not a

connection: they cannot see your profile, cannot message you privately, and

you can leave at any time without anyone being told. Every plan shows who

invited whom.

Plans are private. They are never listed, searchable or browsable by anyone

who is not in them.
